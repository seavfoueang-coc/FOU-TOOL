Custom Logo Placement Guide:
============================
You can drop your own logo files directly into this /public folder anytime:

1. HONGGUO DL App Logo:
   - Place as: /public/hongguo-dl-logo.png
   - The app will automatically detect and load your image across all HONGGUO DL cards, badges, and headers!

2. Main Website / FOU TOOL Logo (Title Image):
   - Place as: /public/image.png (or /public/fou-tool-logo.png)
   - The app will automatically detect and load your image in the website title bar, navbar, and hub header!

KHQR Image Placement Guide:
============================
Place your real KHQR bank image in this directory with any of the following names:
1. /public/khqr.png  (Recommended)
2. /public/khqr.jpg
3. /public/khqr.jpeg
4. /public/images/khqr.png
5. /public/images/khqr.jpg

The application will automatically detect and display your real KHQR image!
You can also drag and drop or select your image directly in the web app.
