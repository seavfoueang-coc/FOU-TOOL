KHQR Image Placement Guide:
============================

Place your real KHQR bank image in this directory with any of the following names:
1. /public/khqr.png  (Recommended)
2. /public/khqr.jpg
3. /public/khqr.jpeg
4. /public/images/khqr.png
5. /public/images/khqr.jpg

The application will automatically detect and display your real KHQR image!
You can also drag and drop or select your image directly in the web app.
